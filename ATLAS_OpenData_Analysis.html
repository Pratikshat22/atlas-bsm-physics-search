Key Physics Channels

  • BSM Resonances: Z′→ee, Z′→μμ, Z′→ττ, Z′→tt, Z′→bb, W′→eν, W′→μν, W′→qq at 3 TeV
  • Higgs Production: ggF, VBF, WH, ZH, ttH, tH (σ range: 0.06-28.3 pb)
  • Higgs Decays: γγ, ZZ, WW, ττ, μμ, bb, Zγ (BR range: 0.02-58.2%)
  • SUSY: Gluino, stop, stau, chargino, higgsino (σ range: 0.001-0.03 pb)
  • Rare SM: ttW, ttZ, ttH, ttγ, ttWW, triboson (WWW, WWZ, WZZ, ZZZ)
  • Diphoton: QCD γγ from 55 GeV to 5 TeV (σ range: 85 pb - 1.2×10⁻⁶ pb)

Key Findings

  • Z′ production follows 1/M⁴ scaling as predicted by SSM
  • Higgs cross sections: ggF dominates (28.3 pb), VBF second (3.75 pb)
  • Higgs mainly decays to bb (58%), WW (21%), ττ (6.2%)
  • ttbar production (730 pb) is the dominant top process
  • Diphoton cross section drops by factor 10⁸ from 55 GeV to 5 TeV
  • SUSY cross sections are O(0.001-0.03 pb) requiring high luminosity

This analysis covers the complete ATLAS MC production for Run 2, including all major BSM, Higgs, and SM processes at 13 TeV. The datasets are available for research and education through the ATLAS Open Data platform.
